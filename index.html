The data-center pivot

Unlock stranded compute with stored cooling.

Data centers live under a fixed power cap. On the hottest days, chillers eat that budget — so megawatts that could be sold as compute sit stranded. Nostromo charges thermal storage overnight and discharges it through the afternoon peak, pulling cooling load off the meter exactly when it matters and handing those megawatts back to IT.

❄️

Charge cold at night

Off-peak, with cool ambient air and spare capacity under the cap, chillers freeze the IceBricks store.

▸ COP highest · grid headroom widest
⚡

Free kW on peak

At peak, stored cooling does the work and chillers throttle down — meter drops below the cap.

▸ +MW returned to sellable compute
🛡️

Bridging resilience

Stored cooling can bridge cooling for a short window during a disturbance — buying time, not full backup.

▸ minutes-scale ride-through
Batteries shift load. Nostromo removes it — cooling demand is taken off the meter, not just moved in time.
